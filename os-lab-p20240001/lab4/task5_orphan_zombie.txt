--- Zombie and Orphan Cleanup Notes ---
Q: How are orphans cleaned up? A: They get 'adopted' by a system process, like since every child needs a parent, the OS gives them a new one to handle their final exit
Q: How are zombies cleaned up? A: The parent process has to acknowledge them by calling wait(). If the parent won't do it, the zombie stays stuck until the parent itself dies
Q: Can you kill a zombie with 'kill -9'? A: No, a zombie is just a ghost in the process table so in order to get rid of it, you have to kill its parent instead
